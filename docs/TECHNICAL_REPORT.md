## 1. The Approach: What We Built and Why

### 1.1 First-Principles Diagnosis of `spellF1` Bottlenecks
The official ranking metric (`spellF1`, mean F1 across Hungarian-matched contiguous `H`/`A` spells over IoU thresholds `0.30..0.70`) evaluates **global episode topology** rather than isolated frame accuracy. Our error decomposition on external LOMO validation (`Sportec` and `Metrica Sample`) identified two structural bottlenecks:
- **Bottleneck 1 — The Double-Domino Short-Spell Collapse (`48.2%` of all remaining error, `+0.1164 spellF1` oracle ceiling on external LOMO `J1A`):** When Team $Y$ executes a brief `0.8s–3.5s` interception, controlled clearance, or one-touch transition between two possessions of Team $X$ ($X_1 \to Y \to X_2$), standard frame-level HMM/Viterbi smoothing bridges over $Y$ and merges $X_1$ and $X_2$ into a single macro-spell. Under Hungarian matching, **missing a single short touch causes 3 simultaneous spell errors (`2 FN + 1 FP`)**.
- **Bottleneck 2 — Cross-Provider Feature Scale Shift & Optical Tracking Dropouts:** Automatic optical tracking exhibits ball dropouts (`ball_visible = 0`), camera-crop player absences, occasional team-slot swaps during close duels, and cross-provider feature scale attenuation in unnormalized tree-based transition heads when moving across stadiums and tracking providers.

### 1.2 Our 5-Layer Architecture (`Super-Champion v11 Big-Leap`)
The pipeline is designed around $H \leftrightarrow A$ symmetry and uses leave-one-match-out evaluation on external DFL/Sportec matches. Exact symmetry across the entire final pipeline has not been established by the current harness; the Metrica calibration and selection history are described below:

1. **Layer 1 — Causal Optical Input Cleaning, Automatic Slot-Count Input Check (`D3`) & 3D Ball Physics (`244` Core + `481` `PhysV2` + `517` 3D Columns):**
   - **Broadcast-Assisted Tracking Repair (`repair_atd_match`):** Uses aligned broadcast tracking to fill missing tactical player and ball positions during tactical blackouts. The submission pipeline applies its pre-kickoff and ungraded-frame handling separately; the kickoff boundary remains subject to the check described below.
   - **Automatic Tracking Slot-Count Input Check (`D3`, `players_reteam.py` & `d3_reteam_and_repro.py`):** In automatic optical tracking, close player crossings occasionally swap team slots, leaving an impossible team-count imbalance (`12 vs 10` instead of `11 vs 11`). Players are detected in the provided tactical video with a pre-trained, unmodified YOLO11m detector (Ultralytics, COCO weights), projected to the pitch with the provided homography and matched to ATD coordinates. A rule-based check identifies intervals where the tracking data itself violates the `11 vs 11` count constraint (`count`-corroborated mode, excluding goalkeepers), and the swapped tracklet slot is resolved with a team-colour classifier (`HistGradientBoostingClassifier`) trained on torso-colour features from the video, labelled with ATD team slots on balanced `11 vs 11` frames (no possession labels). This produces an intermediate step file (`local_data/d3_reteam_cache/`) on which all externally trained model families are evaluated.
   - **3D Ball Trajectory & Stoppage Kinematics:** Reconstructs vertical ball kinematics (`ball_z`, `vz`, `az`, aerial flight flags, specific kinetic energy, and stoppage deceleration) from 2D ball acceleration and flight physics.

2. **Layer 2 — 13-Family Symmetry-Designed Ensemble (Supervised Training on External DFL/Sportec Data):**
   - **Families 1–9 (`v8.5` Base & `PhysV2` Core):** `SymCatBoost`, `SymLightGBM`, `SymXGBoost`, `SymTCN`, `SymBiGRU`, and `SymPlayerSetNet` trained across clean (`v3`) and synthetically degraded optical-noise (`v6`) Sportec LOMO folds.
   - **Family 10 — `SymPlayerSetNetV2` (Modal Cloud GPU, External Sportec LOMO):** 3D-ball permutation-invariant set transformer over all 22 players with dedicated aerial-header (`p_aerial`) and 4-directional boundary (`p4d`) heads.
   - **Family 11 — `L1-Trees 517-Col` + 3D Duel/Aerial Heads (Modal Cloud 128-CPU, External Sportec LOMO):** `LightGBM`, `CatBoost`, and `XGBoost` over 517 2D+3D kinematic features plus dedicated short-duel turnover (`l1_duel`) and aerial heads.
   - **Family 12 — `SymTrajectoryEventNetV10` (Modal Cloud `8x NVIDIA A10G` GPUs, External Sportec LOMO):** Spatio-temporal transformer (`PlayerTrajectoryEncoder` + **`WindowLocalNorm1d(window_size=1501)`** + `BallToPlayerCrossAttention`) that encodes each player's continuous trajectory $(x, y, v_x, v_y, a_x, a_y, \kappa)$ before cross-attending to the 3D ball. Achieves **`0.7355` (`J1A`)**, **`0.6984` (`J1B`)**, and **`0.8805` (`J2_noD`)** as a single standalone model (`p_duel_to` ROC-AUC = `0.9576` on external Sportec LOMO).
   - **Family 13 — `SymPhysConformerV11` (Modal Cloud `8x NVIDIA A10G` GPUs, External Sportec LOMO):** Hybrid **Dilated Depthwise-Separable Conv + Multi-Head Self-Attention Conformer** over the 481-column `PhysV2` table with `WindowLocalNorm1d(1501)` and 5 physics-supervised multi-task heads (`proba`, `change`, `p4d`, `p_duel_to`, `p_aerial`). Achieves **`0.6813` standalone `J1B (v6)` `spellF1`** (**`+0.0186` over `SymTCN_PhysV2` and `+0.0386` over `SymBiGRU_PhysV2`**) and `0.9817 / 0.9764` 4D-boundary ROC-AUC (`v3 / v6` external Sportec LOMO).

3. **Layer 3 — `SymUnifiedStreamV11`: Zero-Parameter Input Quantile Normalization & Duel-Modulated Stream Synthesis:**
   - **Zero-Parameter Input Quantile Normalization (`synthesize_calibrated_p4d_v11`):** Just as `LayerNorm` and `WindowLocalNorm1d(1501)` standardize internal neural activations, we apply a parameter-free mathematical input standardization to the 4-directional boundary stream by normalizing against its own unlabeled, $H \leftrightarrow A$-symmetric pooled 99th percentile $q_{99}(\{p_{4d}^{(H)} \cup p_{4d}^{(A)}\})$ on `half > 0` (with **zero learned parameters and zero label/video fitting**), blending it with our 3 Window-Local-Normalized neural 4D boundary heads (`SymPlayerSetNetV2`, `SymTrajectoryEventNetV10`, `SymPhysConformerV11`).
   - **Decoupled 13-Family Stream (`synthesize_unified_stream_v11`):** Preserves exact per-half `P(D)` and `p_change` marginals while injecting an $H \leftrightarrow A$-invariant duel-turnover transition bonus (`g_duel * max(p4d_v11[:, 0], p4d_v11[:, 1])`) so `viterbi_hsmm` lowers its switch penalty during genuine physical steals.

4. **Layer 4 — Fixed-Filter Audio Whistle Input (`W4`), Stoppage Refiner (`W5`) & SmartTagging Restart Input Alignment (`S3`):**
   - Applies a fixed, non-trainable digital bandpass filter (`3.95–4.15 kHz` spectral energy ratio) to extract referee whistle peaks alongside the provided `SmartTag` event timestamps as inference inputs (`StoppageStartRefiner`, `SmartTaggingArbitrator`, and `apply_d3_post_kick_sliver_bridge`), with the origin of its decision thresholds under review.

5. **Layer 5 — `SymBigLeapLatticeV11`: Domain-Invariant Multi-Family Segment-Graph & Dual-Viterbi Arbitrator (Tuned 100% on External Sportec LOMO):**
   - Evaluates candidate short episodes across 5 topological operations using **4-family neural+tree consensus (`v11_cons_pY`, `v11_neural_b4d_geom`, `v11_b4d_geom`, `v11_cons_duel`)** and **analytical Hungarian Double-Domino payoff** with all thresholds fixed on external Sportec LOMO folds:
     - `MBR-v11`: Absorbs false interior micro-islands (`X -> Y -> X`, deflection/ball-dropout artifacts) back into host spell `X`.
     - `XYX-v11`: Carves genuine short interior steals (`X -> Y -> X`, `0.5s–3.5s`) when multi-family neural+tree consensus and physical proximity (`min_d1_Y <= 0.45m`) confirm control.
     - `XYD-v11` & `DYX-DI` (`score_dyx_di`): Resolves pre-stoppage (`X -> Y -> D`) clearances/fouls and post-restart (`D -> Y -> X`) aerial/second-ball turnovers via 3-family exit boundary geometric consensus (`r4a..r4c`).
     - `VIT-DIFF` (`extract_viterbi_discrepancy_candidates_v11`): Compares full-stream Viterbi decoding on the 13-family unified stream against the base stream to rescue sustained multi-family sequence takeovers.
     - `B2-DI` (`SymTouchBoundaryAlignerV11` / `enrich_b2_cand_v11` + `score_offsets_b2_di`): Snaps live `H <-> A` transition boundaries to the exact physical touch impulse frame using `float64` 10-decimal-rounded multi-family (`ps2`, `t10`, `pc11`) 4D boundary consensus (`+12 / -0` across all external validation splits).

### 2.2 Ideas & Assets Tried and **Dropped** (Negative Results)
1. **SkillCorner Open Data (20 Broadcast Matches, MIT License) as Extra Training Data — DROPPED:** Evaluated as an external training source (`experiments/log/037`), but because broadcast tracking only observes `~12` visible players per frame without full-pitch 22-player geometry, adding it degraded external LOMO `spellF1` on both Sportec (`J1`) and Metrica (`J2`).
2. **Public sample and selection — REVIEW REQUIRED:** The final D3 path applies DeadWedge, whose development and selection history must be checked against the rule prohibiting use of the public sample for tuning. Compliance has not yet been established.
3. **Global `InstanceNorm1d` Over Full Match Length in Sequence Models — DROPPED:** Normalizing over a full half mixes dead-ball and live-play variance, causing output scale drift across tracking providers. Replaced by local sliding **`WindowLocalNorm1d(window_size=1501)`** (`60s` window) in `SymTrajectoryEventNetV10` and `SymPhysConformerV11`.
4. **Unstandardized Tree Boundary Scores Across Providers — DROPPED:** Raw GBDT 4-directional transition probabilities exhibit provider-dependent scale differences unless standardized. Fixed in `v11` via zero-parameter pooled quantile standardization (`synthesize_calibrated_p4d_v11`) combined with Window-Local-Normalized neural 4D boundary heads.
5. **Naive Linear Blending of `P(D)` Across Neural Heads — DROPPED:** Shifting the per-half mean of `P(D)` or `p_change` can distort `viterbi_hsmm` emission-to-transition balance. Solved in `synthesize_unified_stream_v11` by decoupling `P(H | Alive)` from `P(D)` and preserving exact per-half `P(D)` and `p_change` marginals.

## 3. The Possession Rules Implemented

Following the competition specification and our initial rules inspection, our algorithm enforces explicit physical and tactical criteria across all grey zones of football possession:
1. **Deflections & One-Touch Blocks vs. Controlled Short Recoveries (`MBR-v11` vs. `XYX-v11` / `VIT-DIFF`):**
   - Brief contacts (`<= 2.2s`) where an opposing player touches or deflects the ball without establishing proximity control (`eff_contact_Y == 0` or `pc11_pX >= 0.74` while host `X` maintains `min_d1_X <= 0.42m`) do not break possession and are absorbed into the host team's ongoing spell (`MBR-v11`).
   - Conversely, when an intercepting player establishes decisive physical proximity (`min_d1_Y <= 0.45m`, `x_win_frames == 0`) and 4-family neural+tree consensus (`v11_cons_pY`, `v11_neural_b4d_geom`, `v11_b4d_geom`) confirms a clean incoming and outgoing transition, `XYX-v11` and `VIT-DIFF` segment a distinct short possession (`X -> Y -> X`).
2. **Loose Balls & Passes in Flight:**
   - Possession continues with the last team in control during passes in flight and uncontested loose balls until either the opposing team establishes controlled contact or play is stopped (`D`).
3. **Pre-Stoppage Clearances/Fouls (`XYD-v11`) & Post-Restart Second Balls (`DYX-DI`):**
   - Captures final touches (`0.5s–2.5s`) where Team $Y$ wins the ball immediately before a whistle/touchline exit (`X -> Y -> D`) or controls the first phase after a set piece (`D -> Y -> X`).
4. **Set Pieces, Restarts & Out-of-Play Boundaries (`DeadWedge` + `StoppageStartRefiner` + `SmartTaggingArbitrator`):**
   - Holds `D` during throw-in, corner, free-kick, and goal-kick preparation (including when the ball is carried or rolled before placement) until the exact release impulse frame (`impulse_mag_d4` acceleration spike + `SmartTag` kick event), and sets frames outside the tracked halves to `D`, emitting zero `X` labels. Officially, video frames `0–11,184`, `90,652–110,807` and `190,198–199,318` are ungraded (`40,462` frames), and frames `110,808–113,740` are `D`; frame `113,741` is `H` in the current CSV. The kickoff boundary and its official interpretation require confirmation.

## 5. Limitations & What We Would Fix With More Time

1. **Joint End-to-End Differentiable Semi-Markov / CRF Training Over All 13 Families:**
   - While `SymPhysConformerV11` and `SymTrajectoryEventNetV10` predict multi-task frame, 4D-boundary, and duel-turnover logits that feed into `SymUnifiedStreamV11` and `SymBigLeapLatticeV11`, backpropagating a differentiable soft-Hungarian `spellF1` loss directly through the neural backbones and semi-Markov transition matrix on external Sportec data would further reduce sub-second boundary offset errors (`B2`).
2. **Off-Camera Player Trajectory Completion via Spatio-Temporal Graph Diffusion:**
   - During tight camera crops (`visible_H < 10`), off-camera players temporarily leave the tracking frame. Training a 22-player spatio-temporal diffusion bridge on external full-pitch tracking (DFL/Sportec) conditioned on visible players' velocity and orientation vectors would further improve possession attribution during off-camera aerial duels.
